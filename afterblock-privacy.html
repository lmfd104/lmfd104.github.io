Privacy Policy

AfterBlock: Slam & Slide (Android app, package com.aftershock)  ·  Developer: MiamiJambo  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Short version: AfterBlock: Slam & Slide requires no account and collects no personal data itself. Your scores and progress stay on your device. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes a device advertising identifier under the consent choices you make in the app.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the Android game AfterBlock: Slam & Slide (package name com.aftershock), published on Google Play by the developer account MiamiJambo. Contact: mjambogames@gmail.com.

2. Data the app collects

The game itself collects no personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDKs operated by us. Scores, coins, unlocked themes, settings, and game progress are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows interstitial and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier and related signals to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), the app first shows a Google-certified consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) and honors your choice between personalized and non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw consent at any time from the game's settings. Ad content is rated PG or lower. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. After the purchase, the app stops requesting ads.

5. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; ad content is restricted to a PG-or-lower rating, and consent-based ad personalization applies as described above.

6. Data sharing and deletion

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (advertising, section 3) and Google Play Billing (purchases, section 4). Since all game data is stored locally, uninstalling the app deletes it. To reset the advertising identifier, use your device's Google settings ("Delete advertising ID").

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

Questions? Email mjambogames@gmail.com.
