Privacy Policy

Aftershock Blocks (iOS app, bundle com.aftershock.ios)  ·  Developer: MiamiJambo  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Short version: Aftershock Blocks collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no network connection. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the iOS game Aftershock Blocks (bundle identifier com.aftershock.ios), published on the App Store by MiamiJambo. Contact: mjambogames@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no user accounts and no sign-in. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting services operated by us or by a third party.

3. Data stored on your device

Your high score, coins, unlocked themes, and game settings are saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

4. Network access

The app does not require or use an internet connection. It makes no network requests. Optional platform features such as Game Center leaderboards and cloud save are not enabled in this version.

5. Advertising and purchases

This version of the app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. No advertising identifier is requested or used, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because the app does not track you.

6. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, no personal information is collected from children or from anyone else.

7. Data sharing and deletion

We have no data to share, sell, or disclose. Because all game data is stored locally, deleting the app permanently removes it from your device.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions? Email mjambogames@gmail.com.
