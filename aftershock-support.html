Aftershock Blocks is a single-player block puzzle game. Drop pieces onto the grid, clear full lines, and keep the board alive as long as you can.

How do I play?

Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Filling a complete row or column clears it and scores points. The game ends when none of the pieces in the tray will fit.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Aftershock Blocks runs entirely offline. There is no sign-in, no account, and no network connection required to play.

Where is my progress stored?

Your high score, coins, unlocked themes, and settings are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. This version of Aftershock Blocks contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

I lost my progress / the game won't start

Try closing and reopening the app. If the problem continues, email mjambogames@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version, and we'll help.
