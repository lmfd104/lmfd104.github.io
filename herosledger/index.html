Pathfinder 2e character builder

Hero's Ledger

A complete offline character builder for Pathfinder 2nd Edition. Build your hero, level with confidence, and only ever see the options you can actually take.

Google PlayApp Store

Free. No ads, no account, no sign-in.

What it does

  • All 27 classesCorrect hit points, saving-throw progression, proficiency tracks and class features at every level.
  • 5,900+ featsAncestry, class, skill, general and archetype — searchable, with prerequisites checked in real time.
  • 1,700+ spellsRank, traditions, casting time, components, range, area and duration, with the right slot table for your caster.
  • 5,600+ itemsWeapons with damage and range, armour with AC and Dex caps, plus a dedicated tab for wands and staves.
  • Level-up that knows the schedulePer-class timing for class, skill, general and ancestry feats — including the rogue and investigator exceptions.
  • Dice roller built inAttacks and damage, with critical doubling handled by the PF2e rules.
Something missing or wrong? There's a Report a Bug button in the app's settings, and the links below reach the same place. Bug reports and requests are what set the roadmap.
