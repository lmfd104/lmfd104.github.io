About

These are made by one person, and the same three rules apply to all of them: they work without an internet connection, they don't carry ads, and they don't ask you to make an account before you can start. The rules content in the character builders is generated from the publishers' own published data rather than typed in by hand, which is why it stays current and why the numbers on the sheet can be traced back to a source.

Found a bug, or want something added? Every app has a Report a Bug button in its settings, and each has its own support page and Discord — the links are in the footer. Feedback genuinely sets the roadmap.
