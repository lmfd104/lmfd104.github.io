About Miami Jambo

An independent one-person music and video studio. KPlop is its flagship project: original, bilingual parody K-pop about the way we all behave online.

The idea

K-pop is the most polished pop music on earth, and the internet is the least dignified place on earth. KPlop puts them in the same room. Each single takes one behaviour you have absolutely exhibited this week — manifesting a situationship, doomscrolling at 3AM, ordering the garlic noodles on a first date — and gives it the full idol treatment: a hook, a concept, a group, cover art and a video.

The result sits somewhere between a joke and an actual bop, which is the target. If the song is only funny once, it failed. The chorus has to survive the punchline.

How a single gets made

  • Concept. One trend, one angle, one title that works in both Korean and English.
  • Lyrics. Written bilingually: Korean verses carrying the story, an English hook that lands for listeners who do not speak Korean, and a mirrored translation woven through so both audiences get the same joke at the same moment.
  • The act. An invented group is designed to front the song — name, member count, concept, colour palette. See the acts.
  • Production. The track is produced with AI music tooling, then auditioned across multiple takes; the strongest performance is chosen, trimmed and mastered by hand.
  • Cover art. Each release gets its own key art in the group's palette, used as the video backdrop and the thumbnail.
  • Karaoke subtitles. Word-level timings are extracted from the finished vocal and burned into the video, so both the Korean and the English scroll in time. This is the part that takes the longest and the part people mention most.
  • Publishing. Every release ships twice: a full-length video and a vertical cut for Shorts and TikTok.

AI, honestly

KPlop is openly an AI-assisted project. Concepts, lyrics, structure, arrangement direction, editing and the final choice of every take are human decisions; music generation and cover art use AI tooling. Nothing here is presented as the work of a real performer, and every act is labelled fictional on the acts page and in each video's description.

Publishing and automation

Miami Jambo publishes to its own channels through the official platform APIs — the YouTube Data API for videos and Shorts, and the TikTok Content Posting API for the vertical cuts. The tooling exists for one reason: a release means uploading the same video, title, description and caption set to several places at once, and doing that by hand is how metadata ends up inconsistent.

The integration is strictly first-party. It authenticates as Miami Jambo, uploads only videos this studio created, and posts only to Miami Jambo's own accounts. It does not read, collect, scrape or republish anyone else's content, and it is not offered to third parties as a service. See the privacy policy for exactly what data that involves.

The parody line

Every group, idol, lyric and storyline in KPlop is original and fictional. No real artist, song, agency or label is sampled, named, impersonated or implied, and the project is not affiliated with or endorsed by anyone in the industry it is affectionately making fun of.
