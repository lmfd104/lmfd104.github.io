The acts

Every KPlop single is credited to its own invented group. None of them are real: no agency, no trainees, no fan meets. They exist so each song can have its own concept, colour palette and attitude.

A bubblegum anthem for manifesting a one-sided situationship into a full relationship through sheer delusional confidence. "He left me on read — that's basically a proposal."

Why fictional acts?

Because parody works best when nobody real is the punchline.

K-pop's group concepts are half the fun — the colour schemes, the lore, the rookie-of-the-year narrative. KPlop borrows the format and invents everything inside it. Every group name, member count and concept on this page was written for the song it fronts.

That also keeps the project clean: the songs satirise internet behaviour, never a real performer. No existing artist, agency or label is referenced, sampled or implied, and nothing here should be read as being about, endorsed by, or affiliated with anyone real.
