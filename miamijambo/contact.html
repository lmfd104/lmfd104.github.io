Frequently asked

Are these real K-pop groups?

No. Every act is invented for the song it fronts, and every lyric is original. KPlop is a parody project — see the acts.

Can I use a KPlop song in my video?

Email first. Short-form use with credit and a link back is usually fine; commercial use needs written permission. The terms of service cover the details.

Are the songs on Spotify or Apple Music?

Not yet. Everything currently lives on the YouTube channel as full videos and Shorts, plus the vertical cuts on TikTok.

Do you collect any of my data?

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no accounts. Full detail in the privacy policy.

Something here is wrong or infringes my rights — who do I tell?

Email mjambogames@gmail.com with the specifics and it will be reviewed and, where warranted, taken down. Rights and takedown handling is described in the terms.
