Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Miami Jambo ("we", "us"), the entertainment project behind the KPlop parody-music channel, handles information in connection with this website (lmfd104.github.io/miamijambo/) and our use of third-party platform APIs to publish our own content.

The short version: This is a personal creative project. We do not collect personal information from visitors to this website, we do not run ads or trackers here, and we do not sell data. Our TikTok integration is used only to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account — it never accesses data about other TikTok users.

Information we collect from website visitors

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. Our host (GitHub) may automatically log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability; that data is handled under GitHub's privacy statement and is not accessed or retained by us.

Our use of the TikTok API

We use TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API for a single purpose: to let the owner of our own TikTok account publish our original videos. When our account owner authorizes the application:

  • We receive an access token that lets the application upload videos to, and read basic creator information (such as the account nickname and posting eligibility for) that one authorizing account only.
  • We use this access solely to upload and post our own content and to show its upload status.
  • We do not access, collect, store, or process information about any other TikTok users, followers, viewers, or their content.

Access tokens are stored privately by the account owner and are used only to communicate with TikTok. We do not share, sell, or transfer them to any third party.

How information is shared

We do not sell or rent personal information. Content we create is published to the platforms we operate on — such as TikTok and YouTube — and is governed by those platforms' own privacy policies. We share data with third parties only as required to publish our content or to comply with the law.

Data retention and your choices

The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time from TikTok's account settings, which invalidates the token. Because we do not collect visitor data, there is no visitor profile to delete. If you believe we hold information about you and want it removed, contact us and we will address it promptly.

Children's privacy

Our website and content are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
