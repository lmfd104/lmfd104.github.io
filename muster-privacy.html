Privacy Policy

Muster  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Short version: Muster collects no personal data and requires no account. Army lists are stored only on your device. The only network request the app makes is an optional, user-triggered download of publicly available community data.

1. Who we are

Muster is an independent Android application for planning miniature wargame army rosters. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any game publisher.

2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or contact information is collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers are collected or transmitted by us.
  • No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to any server.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.

3. How the app stores data

All army lists and imported game data you create are stored locally on your device in a private app database. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

4. Internet access — BSData import

Muster requires internet access for one purpose only: downloading community-maintained game data files from BSData (a public open-source project on GitHub). This download is:

  • Optional — the app functions without it, though browsing requires imported data.
  • Triggered only by you — Muster never downloads data automatically or in the background.
  • Directed to a public GitHub URL only — no data is sent to us.

After import, all data is stored locally and the app works fully offline.

5. In-app purchases — Muster Pro

Muster offers an optional one-time purchase ("Muster Pro") to unlock unlimited army lists. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, store, or process any payment card details or billing information. Google's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment data: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Third-party services

Other than Google Play Billing (described above), the app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social login providers.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users, as described above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change our data practices, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
