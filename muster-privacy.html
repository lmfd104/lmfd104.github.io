Privacy Policy

Muster  ·  Last updated: November 2026

Short version: Muster needs no account and collects no personal data. Your collection, hobby log, recipes, game sheets and lists are stored only on your device. The one exception is Rate My List, an optional Pro feature that sends the list you choose to rate for analysis.

1. Who we are

Muster is an independent hobby and army tracker for miniature tabletop wargames. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any game publisher, and it ships no publisher data.

2. Data we collect

No personal data. Muster has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.

3. Data stored on your device

Your models, painting progress, hobby log, paint recipes, game sheets, army lists, campaigns and settings are stored locally on your device. They leave your device only through features you explicitly use, such as exporting a CSV backup or sharing a file yourself. Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Network

Muster works offline. Game sheets are imported from files you choose on your device; the app downloads no game data. It connects to the internet only for:

  • Purchases. Muster Pro is bought through Google Play Billing, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details.
  • Rate My List (optional, Pro). When you tap it, the list you are rating (army name, points and the units in it, plus an opponent's list and your recent results if you add them) is sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the analysis. It contains no personal information, and we do not store it.
  • Feedback on an analysis (optional). If you rate an analysis as helpful or not, we store that answer, any note you type, and the army names involved, to improve the feature. It is not linked to you or your device.

5. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
