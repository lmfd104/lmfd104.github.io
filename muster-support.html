Muster is an independent army-list builder for miniature tabletop wargames.

How do I add units and rules?

Muster ships empty. From the Import screen, load a publicly available community dataset file, and your unit and rules data will appear in the app.

Where is my data stored?

All army lists and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

What does Muster Pro unlock?

Muster Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the free-tier limit so you can save unlimited army lists.
