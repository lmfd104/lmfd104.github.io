Muster is an independent hobby and army tracker for miniature tabletop wargames.

How do I track my models?

Open the Hobby tab and add models by name, army and count. Mark them built, painted and based as you go; progress is logged automatically, and you can log painting sessions by hand too.

How do I add a game, units and points?

Muster ships no publisher data. On the Games tab, tap Save a blank template, fill in your game's armies and units in any spreadsheet app, save it as CSV, then tap Import a game sheet. Try the example game shows the format.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device. Use Back up to a file in the Hobby tab's menu to export a CSV file you can keep or move to a new phone.

What does Muster Pro unlock?

Muster Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the free-tier limits (unlimited army lists and paint recipes) and adds Rate My List, matchup analysis, list exports and a printable PDF, play-mode tracking, and a shopping list of models you still need.
