Hobby & army tracker for tabletop wargames

Muster

A hobby companion for miniature wargamers. Track what you own, what you've painted and the lists you play, for whichever games you play. No publisher data is bundled — you bring your own.

Google Play

Free, with an optional one-off upgrade.

What it does

  • Track your pileAdd models in seconds and mark them built, painted and based as you go. A mixed squad is fine: 12 owned, 7 built, 3 painted.
  • A hobby log that fills itselfProgress you mark is logged automatically. See models painted per month, your streak, and whether your pile is shrinking or growing.
  • Paint recipesSave step-by-step recipes and attach them to the models you used them on.
  • Any game, your dataDescribe a game in a simple spreadsheet sheet and import it, then build points lists against your own units.
  • Genuinely offlineEverything lives on your phone, with a CSV backup you control. No account needed.
  • Native, not a wrapped websiteBuilt for Android directly, so it opens instantly and stays responsive.
Something missing or wrong? There's a Report a Bug button in the app's settings, and the links below reach the same place. Bug reports and requests are what set the roadmap.
