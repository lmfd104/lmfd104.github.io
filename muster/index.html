Army list builder for tabletop wargames

Muster

A free army-planning companion for miniature wargamers. Nothing is bundled — import the community-maintained data for the games you actually play.

Google PlayApp Store

Free, with an optional one-off upgrade.

What it does

  • Eleven game systemsWarhammer 40,000 (10th, 11th and 2nd edition retro), Kill Team 2024, The Horus Heresy, Legions Imperialis, Adeptus Titanicus, Aeronautica Imperialis, Battlefleet Gothic, Age of Sigmar, Warcry and Blood Bowl.
  • Detachment rules, evaluatedNot just a points total — the modifiers your chosen detachment actually imposes on what you can take.
  • Straight from BSDataThe open-source data project the community already maintains, fetched on demand and kept current.
  • Genuinely offlineImport once over wi-fi and the whole app works in a basement, a game store, or anywhere else with no signal.
  • Full stat blocksTap any unit for every profile, ability and keyword — enough to judge a choice without a second tab open.
  • Native, not a wrapped websiteBuilt for Android and iOS directly, so it opens instantly and stays responsive.
Something missing or wrong? There's a Report a Bug button in the app's settings, and the links below reach the same place. Bug reports and requests are what set the roadmap.
