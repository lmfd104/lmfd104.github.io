Delete Your Account

Mythloom  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Summary: You can permanently delete your Mythloom account and all associated data at any time, either from within the app or by emailing us.

1. Delete from within the app

  1. Open Mythloom and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and associated data are permanently removed from our systems.

2. Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, email mjambogames@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify ownership and process the deletion.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identifier (email).
  • Characters, campaigns, party membership, and session text you created.
  • App activity associated with your account.

4. What may be retained

Shared campaign content that other players contributed to remains available to those players. We may retain limited records where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, or in anonymized/aggregated form that is no longer linked to you. Purchase and subscription records held by Google Play are governed by Google's own policies.

5. Timing

Deletion requests are processed promptly and completed within 30 days.

6. Contact

Questions about account deletion? Email mjambogames@gmail.com.
