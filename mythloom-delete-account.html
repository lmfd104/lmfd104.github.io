Delete Your Account

Mythloom  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Summary: You can permanently delete your Mythloom account and all associated data at any time, either from within the app or by emailing us.

1. Delete from within the app

  1. Open Mythloom.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

Deletion is immediate: your account and associated data are permanently removed from our systems, and the app returns to a fresh state.

2. Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, email lmfd104@gmail.com from the address associated with your account (or include your in-app display name and party code) and ask us to delete it. We will verify ownership and process the deletion.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identifier (anonymous ID, plus email if you linked one).
  • Your display name, everywhere it appears.
  • Your characters (retired and scrubbed of the details you wrote).
  • Your secret actions and private Game Master messages (erased).
  • Your party memberships and app activity counters.

4. What may be retained

Story turns already woven into a shared campaign remain readable by the other players in that campaign, but with your identity removed — your name is gone and the text can no longer be linked to you. We may retain limited records where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Purchase and subscription records held by Google Play are governed by Google's own policies.

5. Timing

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Email requests are processed promptly and completed within 30 days.

6. Contact

Questions about account deletion? Email lmfd104@gmail.com.
