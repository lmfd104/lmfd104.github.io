Mythloom is a multiplayer tabletop-style adventure where an AI Game Master narrates your party's story. Gather 1–5 players, forge characters, and weave your legend together.

How do I start a game?

Create a party from the Home screen and share the party code with your friends, or join a party with a code someone shares with you. The owner picks a world and pacing, and the Game Master takes it from there.

How do turns work?

Each turn, every player submits an action. Once the party is in (or the pacing deadline passes), the Game Master resolves the turn and narrates what happens. Pacing can be Live, Daily, or Relaxed — the party owner can change it in campaign settings.

Is my story content sent to an AI service?

Yes — the Game Master is powered by a large language model. The actions and character details you submit in a campaign are processed by our backend and shared with our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate the story. See the privacy policy for details.

Follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Content rating

Mythloom keeps stories within a PG-13 ceiling. The Game Master will steer scenes away from explicit content.
