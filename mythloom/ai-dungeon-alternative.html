Looking for an AI Dungeon alternative?

AI Dungeon invented the genre, and a decade of its players have named the two things they still want: an AI that remembers the story it's telling, and a way to play it with friends, like a real table. Mythloom was built for exactly those two.

Memory is the whole game

Freeform text AIs forget: the context window slides, and the innkeeper who owed you a favor in chapter one is a stranger by chapter three. Mythloom's Game Master keeps structured memory instead:

  • Entity cards — every significant NPC, place and threat is tracked as data, not prose.
  • Plot threads — the GM records the promises its story makes and is steered back to pay them off.
  • Cross-campaign world memory — finish a campaign and the world remembers your party in the next one.

A table, not a text box

  • Parties of 1–5 with a six-letter invite code. One story, everyone shapes it.
  • Async turns — each player answers on their own schedule; the GM weaves the round when the party's in. Or go live-paced at a real table.
  • Secret moves — act unseen; the shared narration never betrays you. Your rogue's business stays your rogue's business.
  • Real dice — a deterministic engine rolls every d20 and owns the rules. The AI narrates; it can't fudge.
  • One Pro host, the whole party plays free — $9.99/mo covers up to five players. A real free tier (20 GM turns/week) if nobody upgrades.
  • Spoken narration and hands-free voice play, in English or German.

What Mythloom is not

It's not an anything-goes text sandbox. Stories hold to a PG-13 ceiling, worlds are curated (dark-fantasy Emberfall, sci-fi Drift Sector), and characters are built with real attributes, callings and talents — closer to a tabletop campaign than a blank prompt. If you want unbounded freeform prose, the classics have you covered. If you want a game, pull up a chair.
