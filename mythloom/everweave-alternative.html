The short version

If you mostly play alone and love Everweave's flavor, it remains a good app. If you want to bring friends — one subscription for the whole table, secrets between players, turns that resolve on everyone's own schedule, and a GM whose memory is a database rather than a fading context window — that's the table Mythloom set.
