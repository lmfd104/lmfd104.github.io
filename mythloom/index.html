Multiplayer AI Game Master

Mythloom

A tabletop-style adventure where an AI Game Master narrates a story built around the choices you and your friends make. No rulebooks, no scheduling.

Google PlayApp Store

Free to play. One Pro host covers the whole party.

What it does

  • Play together or aloneShare a six-letter code and up to five adventurers drop straight into your story — or start a solo campaign with the same depth.
  • Turns on your own timeEveryone submits an action when they can; the GM weaves the round once the party is in.
  • Two worlds to startEmberfall, for swords and old roads. Drift Sector, for salvage crews on the edge of space.
  • A GM that remembersStructured memory — entity cards, plot threads and world state that carry across sessions.
  • Secret movesAct without the rest of the party seeing it. Nobody else reads that text.
  • Your party plays freeOne Pro host covers the table, so the cost never scales with the number of friends you bring.
