One subscription covers the whole table

Mythloom is free to play, solo or multiplayer. Upgrade one host to Pro ($9.99/mo or $99.99/yr) and the entire party — up to five adventurers — plays the sharper Game Master free. Every other AI RPG charges each player separately.

A Game Master that remembers

  • Entity memory — NPCs keep their names, grudges, and promises across dozens of turns.
  • Open plot threads — the GM tracks the promises its own story has made, so a hook planted in turn 3 pays off in turn 20.
  • World memory — the world remembers your party's deeds across campaigns.
  • Every turn ends on a cliffhanger — and that open question greets you when you come back.

“Will Brannock step into the line the Guild and Wardens are both dancing around?”
— an actual turn-ending hook from a live campaign

Secret moves

Whisper an action the rest of the party can’t see. The shared story never gives you away — the consequences surface later, in the fiction, where they belong. No other AI game master does per-player secrets in multiplayer.

Real dice, honest math

A deterministic engine rolls every d20 and applies every rule — the AI narrates, it never fudges. Spend Fate to reroll a failure at the worst moment; earn Influence by letting others lead.

Built for real groups

  • Parties of 1–5 with a six-letter invite code — friends drop straight in.
  • Async turns: play at lunch, on the bus, whenever. Live pacing when everyone's around.
  • The GM speaks its narration aloud — or reads silently. Hands-free voice mode included.
  • Narration in English or German — your party picks.
  • Two worlds at launch: Emberfall (dark fantasy) and Drift Sector (salvage-crew sci-fi).
