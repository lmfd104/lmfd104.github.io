Privacy Policy

Hero's Ledger  ·  Last updated: June 2025

Short version: Hero's Ledger collects no personal data, requires no account, and works entirely offline. Nothing leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Hero's Ledger is an independent Android application for building Pathfinder 2nd Edition characters. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Paizo Inc.

2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or contact information is collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers are collected or transmitted.
  • No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to any server.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.

3. How the app stores data

All character data you create is stored locally on your device in a private app database. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

4. Internet access

Hero's Ledger does not require an internet connection and does not make any network requests. The app functions entirely offline.

5. Third-party services

The app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social login providers.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users, as described above.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change our data practices, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
