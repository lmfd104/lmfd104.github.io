Privacy Policy

SF2e Builder  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Short version: SF2e Builder collects no personal data, requires no account, and stores everything only on your device. Nothing you create leaves your device.

1. Who we are

SF2e Builder is an independent Android application for building characters for the Starfinder 2nd Edition tabletop roleplaying game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Paizo Inc.

2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or contact information is collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers are collected or transmitted by us.
  • No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to any server.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.

3. How the app stores data

All character data you create (names, statistics, equipment, feats, and choices) is stored locally on your device in a private app database. Backups you create are also written to local files on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

4. Internet access — app updates

SF2e Builder is designed to work offline. The only network activity the app performs is checking for and downloading app updates through Google Play in-app updates, a standard feature provided by Google Play. This is handled entirely by Google Play services and sends none of your character data. Google's privacy policy governs how Google Play handles this: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Third-party services

Other than Google Play (described above), the app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social login providers.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users, as described above.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change our data practices, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
