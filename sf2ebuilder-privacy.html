Privacy Policy

Starfarer's Ledger  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Short version: Starfarer's Ledger collects no personal data and requires no account. Everything you create is stored only on your device.

1. Who we are

Starfarer's Ledger is an independent character builder for the second-edition science-fantasy tabletop role-playing game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any game publisher.

2. Data we collect

None. Starfarer's Ledger does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

Characters, lists, settings, and any content you create are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device except through features you explicitly invoke (for example, exporting or sharing a file yourself). Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Network

Starfarer's Ledger does not transmit your characters or any other data anywhere. On Android, the app may check Google Play for app updates (handled entirely by Google Play); apart from that it makes no network requests and works offline.

5. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
