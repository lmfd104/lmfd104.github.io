SF2e Builder is an independent character builder for the second-edition science-fantasy tabletop role-playing game.

How do I create a character?

Tap the + button on the Home screen and follow the guided step-by-step wizard. Prerequisites are enforced automatically so your build stays legal.

Where is my data stored?

All characters and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is required.

How do I export a character sheet?

Open a character and use the export option to produce a filled, print-ready sheet.
