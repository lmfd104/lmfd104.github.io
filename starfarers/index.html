Starfinder 2e character builder

Starfarer's Ledger

Build a Starfinder 2e character in minutes, level it up with guided choices, and run it at the table from a sheet that shows its working.

Google PlayApp Store

Free. No ads, no account, no sign-in.

What it does

  • Guided creationAncestry, heritage, background, class, attributes, skills, feats and equipment — one step at a time, with your prerequisites checked as you go.
  • A sheet that explains itselfTap any stat — AC, saves, skills, class DC — and see exactly which bonuses produced the number.
  • Conditions that actually applyFrightened, clumsy, enfeebled and drained adjust your stats the moment you set them.
  • Every Player Core classWith their signature choices intact — mystic connections, witchwarper paradox, and the rest.
  • Export and back upThe official Starfinder 2e PDF, a clean custom sheet, or JSON to a file, Drive or Dropbox.
  • Built for any screenA two-pane layout on tablets, compact on phones, and no internet required at any point.
Something missing or wrong? There's a Report a Bug button in the app's settings, and the links below reach the same place. Bug reports and requests are what set the roadmap.
