Privacy Policy

Tallyside for iOS  ·  Last updated: August 2026

Short version: Tallyside for iOS collects no personal data and requires no account. Everything you record is stored only on your device.
App identity: Tallyside  ·  bundle ID com.flagstatspro  ·  developer MiamiJambo. Tallyside was formerly named “TeamTally”, and “FlagStats Pro” before that, which is why the bundle ID and the address of this page still read flagstats and teamtally. The app, the developer, and this policy are the same throughout.

1. Who we are

Tallyside is an independent stat-keeping app for youth sports teams — rosters, schedules, live game scoring, and season summaries. It is published by MiamiJambo under the bundle ID com.flagstatspro.

2. Data we collect

None. Tallyside for iOS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

Teams, rosters, schedules, game scores, and stats you record are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device except through features you explicitly invoke (for example, sharing a game recap yourself). Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Network

The iOS app makes no network requests. It is fully offline.

5. Children's privacy

The app is designed for coaches and parents. It collects no data from anyone, including children. Player names you enter stay on your device.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
