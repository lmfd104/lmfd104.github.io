TeamTally is a stat-keeping app for youth sports teams: manage your roster, plan the season schedule, score games live, and review season stats.

How do I set up my team?

Open the Roster screen to add players, then add games on the Schedule screen. Everything else builds from there.

How do I score a game?

Open a scheduled game and use the live scoring screen to record plays as they happen. The game summary and season stats update automatically.

Where is my data stored?

All teams, schedules, and stats are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is required.
