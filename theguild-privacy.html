Privacy Policy

The Guild: Emberfall for iOS  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Short version: The Guild: Emberfall collects no personal data and requires no account. Your guild lives only on your device.

1. Who we are

The Guild: Emberfall is an independent single-player management RPG — you run an adventurers' guild: accept contracts, train and equip heroes, and hold the realm against dungeon outbreaks.

2. Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

Your save games, settings, and cosmetic unlocks are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app removes this data. The in-game save export feature produces a file you control.

4. In-app purchases

Optional cosmetic purchases (such as portrait style packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Purchase processing is handled by RevenueCat's SDK solely to validate and restore your purchases; see RevenueCat's privacy policy. We receive no payment details and no personal information.

5. Network

Beyond purchase validation when you buy or restore a purchase, the game makes no network requests. It is fully playable offline.

6. Children's privacy

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
