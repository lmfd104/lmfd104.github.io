For help, bug reports, or feedback, email mjambogames@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my save? Saves are stored on your device and back up automatically with your normal device backup. The Guild menu also offers a manual save export/import.

How do I restore a purchase? Guild → Portrait Styles → Restore Purchases (sign in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase).

The game lost my progress. Reinstalling after a device backup restore should recover your guild; if not, email us — an exported save file can be re-imported from the Guild menu.
